Dear Bending Spoons Team,

I’m applying for the UX/UI Designer role because I enjoy working on digital experiences and solving problems through design.

My background started in branding and web design, then moved into UX/UI, front-end implementation, content management, analytics, and digital projects.

During my experience I worked on websites, landing pages, corporate platforms, and recently also on internal tools and workflows.

One project that was important for me was the creation of an internal tool for product data preparation. The goal was to simplify a difficult process where product information came from PDFs, screenshots, and technical catalogs and had to be converted into a format compatible with a legacy backend.

Instead of changing the backend, I created a workflow with AI-assisted extraction, data validation, normalization, and manual review to reduce errors and make the process easier.

This project helped me understand that I like working not only on interfaces, but also on systems, workflows, and problem solving.

I’m interested in Bending Spoons because I would like to work in an environment with high standards, strong product culture, and opportunities to learn from experienced people.

I believe I could be a good fit because I combine design, technical understanding, implementation experience, and curiosity.

Thank you for your consideration.

Best regards,
